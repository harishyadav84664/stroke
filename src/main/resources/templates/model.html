The diagnostic model

Innovation in stroke identification

A machine learning based diagnostic model using neuroimages

NIDUS is the reading-room front for that model. It keeps a MySQL worklist of neuroimage studies, shows a call (ischemic, hemorrhagic, or no acute stroke), and leaves the final read with a person.
